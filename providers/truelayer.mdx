TrueLayer is a UK Open Banking provider. Its Data API reads current accounts, savings accounts, and credit cards from most UK banks and card issuers. You bring your own TrueLayer app, so Sure connects to TrueLayer directly with your credentials.

The TrueLayer connection flow is available in English and Indonesian.

See [Transaction history and import limits](/providers/transaction-history) for initial sync defaults, history limits, and options for older transactions.

## How it works

TrueLayer uses the UK Open Banking framework to give Sure read-only access to your accounts:

1. Create a TrueLayer app and obtain a client ID and client secret
2. Add your credentials to Sure's TrueLayer provider settings
3. Connect a bank through TrueLayer's authorization flow
4. Sure imports your accounts and begins syncing balances and transactions
5. Data refreshes automatically to keep your information current

Your bank credentials are never shared with TrueLayer or Sure. All authentication happens directly with your bank.

## Setting up TrueLayer

### Step 1: Create a TrueLayer app

1. Sign up at [console.truelayer.com](https://console.truelayer.com) and create an app
2. Copy the **client ID** and **client secret**
3. Under **Allowed redirect URIs**, add the callback URL shown in Sure's TrueLayer panel. It is `https://<your-sure-host>/truelayer_items/callback` and must match exactly. TrueLayer accepts plain `http` only for `localhost`, so a public instance requires HTTPS.

<Note>
  A client ID starting with `sandbox-` uses TrueLayer's sandbox environment, which includes a **Mock Bank** provider for testing without a real account.
</Note>

### Step 2: Add credentials to Sure

1. In Sure, go to **Settings > Providers** and open the **TrueLayer** panel
2. Enter your client ID and client secret, then save

### Step 3: Connect a bank

1. Click **Connect a bank** in the TrueLayer panel
2. Pick your bank in TrueLayer's authorization flow and approve access through your bank's app or website
3. After authorization, Sure imports your accounts. Use **Set Up New Accounts** on the Accounts page to choose an account type for each one, or link an account to an existing manual account from that account's page.

Each bank you connect is a separate connection with its own consent. Connect additional banks by clicking **Connect a bank** again.

## History and syncing

UK banks release more than 90 days of history only while you are present — in practice, the few minutes after you approve access. Straight after connecting a bank, Sure fetches history a year at a time, back to `TRUELAYER_BACKFILL_YEARS` (default 7 years), until the bank refuses an older range.

<Warning>
  Set up imported accounts within a few minutes of approving access if you want the full transaction history. If the first import runs after the bank's presence window closes, the connection card notes this and offers a **Reconnect** button to open a new authorization.
</Warning>

The setup dialog includes an **Import transactions from** date. Leave it blank to keep all transactions the bank provided. Setting a date excludes older transactions — later syncs and backfills also respect this cutoff.

After the initial import, syncs read linked accounts only, from a week before the last sync and never more than 89 days back. Unattended access is rate limited (typically around four requests per endpoint per day), so rate-limited requests are not retried.

Transactions are matched by TrueLayer's stable transaction ID, so re-syncing never creates duplicates. Card charges are imported as spending and refunds as income. Pending transactions are not imported — they appear once they are booked.

## Reconnecting

UK Open Banking consent lasts 90 days. Sure shows the expiry date in the TrueLayer panel and a **Reconnect** button from two weeks before it lapses, or immediately if the bank revokes access early. Reconnecting keeps the same TrueLayer account IDs, so linked accounts retain their history.

## Configuration

| Variable | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TRUELAYER_REDIRECT_URI` | Derived from the request | Callback URL sent to TrueLayer. Set this when a proxy does not forward the public host and scheme. |
| `TRUELAYER_BACKFILL_YEARS` | `7` | Years of history fetched straight after a bank is connected (1–20). |
