Trade Republic is a European neo-broker that lets you buy stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, and private market assets. Sure connects to Trade Republic directly using its authenticated web API — no third-party aggregator is required.

## How it works

Sure authenticates to Trade Republic using the same web login flow as the Trade Republic web app. After you confirm the login on your phone, Sure stores an encrypted session token and uses it for all future syncs. Your PIN or password is never stored. It is used only to start a new login session and is discarded immediately after. The credential is also filtered from request logs so it never appears in server-side log output.

Sure discovers two accounts from your Trade Republic connection:

- **Portfolio account** — holds your securities positions (stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, private market instruments) and tracks their current value
- **Cash account** — holds your Trade Republic cash balance and records deposits, withdrawals, card payments, interest, and dividends

## Logging in

Sure supports two login methods:

### Phone number and PIN or password

1. Enter your Trade Republic phone number and your PIN or password
2. Sure starts a web login session with Trade Republic
3. Trade Republic sends a push notification or authenticator code to confirm the login
4. Confirm the login in the Trade Republic app, then click **Confirm login** in Sure

### QR code

1. Click **QR login** in the Trade Republic panel
2. Sure fetches a QR code from Trade Republic
3. Open the Trade Republic mobile app and scan the QR code
4. Sure detects the approved session automatically

Use the QR login if you prefer not to enter your PIN or password in Sure, or if you do not have the Trade Republic authenticator set up.

## Setting up Trade Republic

1. In Sure, go to **Settings > Providers**
2. Find the **Trade Republic** section and click to expand it
3. Choose your login method (phone number and PIN or password, or QR code) and complete the login
4. After a successful login, click **Sync** to discover your accounts
5. Once the sync finishes, click **Set up accounts** to link the discovered portfolio and cash accounts to Sure accounts

If you already have a manual investment account in Sure, you can link the discovered Trade Republic portfolio account to it instead of creating a new one.

## Multiple connections

Sure supports more than one Trade Republic connection per family. Each connection is shown as a separate card in the Trade Republic panel. To add another connection, click **Add another connection** at the bottom of the Trade Republic section and complete the login for the second account.

Each connection card shows a short identifier to help you tell them apart. For phone number logins, Sure displays the last four digits of the phone number. For QR-code logins with no phone number on file, Sure falls back to the last four characters of the linked brokerage account number once a sync has run.

If you complete a login and Sure detects that the Trade Republic account is already linked through another connection in the same family, the new connection is discarded automatically and you will see a notice explaining the duplicate was removed.

## Account types

| Account | Sure account type | What it tracks |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio | Investment | Securities positions and their current value |
| Cash | Depository | Cash balance, deposits, withdrawals, card payments, interest, dividends |

The portfolio account category follows the assets held. Sure maps Trade Republic's portfolio categories to account subtypes:

| Trade Republic category | Sure account subtype |
|---|---|
| Stocks and ETFs | Brokerage |
| Private markets | Private markets |
| Interest / bonds | Interest products |
| Crypto | Crypto wallet |

## What gets imported

### Portfolio account

- Current holdings with quantities and market values
- Trade history (buys and sells), including fees recorded separately on each trade
- Savings plan executions, including historical savings-plan invoices
- Saveback reinvestments (recorded as portfolio trades)
- Round Up investments (recorded as portfolio trades)

### Cash account

- Cash balance
- Deposits and withdrawals, including Google Pay deposits (recorded as standard transactions; automatic transfer matching turns a deposit into a transfer when the counterpart bank account is also in Sure)
- Card payments and ATM withdrawals
- Card refunds
- Interest payouts (labeled Interest)
- Dividends, including per-share amount and withholding tax where available (labeled Dividend)
- Fees (labeled Fee)
- Tax refunds
- Trade settlements: each buy and sell settlement records a cash outflow or inflow linked to the portfolio by a confirmed transfer when both accounts are linked
- Round Up outflows (labeled Buy, linked to the portfolio by a confirmed transfer when both accounts are linked)

When both accounts are linked:

- Buy and sell trades are recorded on the portfolio account. Each trade also produces a corresponding counterpart entry on the portfolio linked to the cash settlement by a confirmed transfer.
- Cash movements (deposits, withdrawals, card payments, interest, dividends) are recorded on the cash account only.
- Saveback events are recorded as portfolio trades only.
- Round Up events produce a portfolio trade and a corresponding cash outflow linked by a confirmed transfer.

## Trade names

Trades are labelled using the format `{ticker} · {quantity}x {name}`. When the security name matches the ticker, the name is omitted: `{ticker} · {quantity}x`. The Buy or Sell direction appears as an activity label rather than in the entry name.

## Securities and market data

Sure resolves holdings and historical trades to exchange tickers when Trade Republic provides a venue symbol. Exchange-listed securities use real ticker symbols for market data lookups. When no usable exchange symbol is available for a position, the security is marked offline and price importers skip it automatically. Sure retries unresolved ISINs at most once per day for up to 30 days.

### Crypto holdings

Trade Republic identifies crypto positions using pseudo-ISINs that encode the coin symbol — for example, `XF000BTC0017` for Bitcoin and `XF000ETH0019` for Ether. When the crypto price provider is enabled in Sure, holdings and trades for these ISINs are resolved to the shared `CRYPTO:<SYMBOL>` security used by on-chain wallets and exchange integrations. Any existing rows stored under the Trade Republic ISIN are migrated over automatically. When the crypto price provider is not enabled, the offline ISIN security is kept and positions remain valued at Trade Republic's own snapshot prices.

Private market positions are valued using quotes from Trade Republic's private markets feed when available. If no live quote exists, Sure uses the average cost basis as the holding value. Other positions without a live price show a price-unavailable indicator rather than a flat return.

## Syncing

Sure syncs Trade Republic accounts on the same schedule as other connected providers. You can also trigger a manual sync from the Trade Republic panel in **Settings > Providers**.

Session tokens are refreshed automatically during each sync when Trade Republic returns a renewed token. If the session expires, the status changes to **Re-authentication required** and you will need to log in again.

### Categories and labels on cash deposits

Categories and activity labels assigned to cash deposits by a Rule are preserved across syncs. Rule-assigned categories and labels on Trade Republic cash deposits are not removed when you sync.

## Troubleshooting

### Re-authentication required

Your Trade Republic session expired. Go to **Settings > Providers > Trade Republic** and log in again using your phone number and PIN or password, or the QR code method.

### Accounts need setup

A sync discovered your Trade Republic accounts, but they have not been linked to Sure accounts yet. Click **Set up accounts** from the Trade Republic panel and complete the account setup.

### No accounts discovered yet

The login succeeded, but no accounts have been found yet. Click **Sync** from the Trade Republic panel and wait for the sync to finish, then try **Set up accounts** again.

### Trade history not showing

If buy or sell trades are missing from your portfolio account, run a manual sync from **Settings > Providers > Trade Republic**. Previous syncs may have skipped order executions due to a locale mismatch — a sync after updating Sure will import them correctly.

If trades are still missing after syncing, check the activity feed on your portfolio account. Trades are recorded in the format `{ticker} · {quantity}x {name}` with Buy or Sell as the activity label.

### Holdings show the wrong value

If a position shows no price or an incorrect value:

- Run a manual sync to refresh market prices
- Check the **Data quality** section on the Trade Republic panel for positions flagged as unpriced
- Private market positions without a live quote are valued at their average cost basis
- Other positions without a live price display a price-unavailable indicator

### Deposits showing as transfers or investment contributions

Earlier syncs classified Trade Republic cash-account deposits as funds movements (which appeared as transfers) or investment contributions. A sync resets those entries to standard transactions automatically, unless you edited them or they are part of a matched transfer. If any deposits still appear incorrectly after syncing, run a manual sync from **Settings > Providers > Trade Republic**.

### Balance does not match the Trade Republic app

Sure uses the booked cash balance from Trade Republic, which matches the balance shown in the Trade Republic app. Funds reserved for open orders (such as pending limit orders) are not subtracted from this balance — those orders have not settled, so no matching transaction exists yet.

If the balance still does not match, a sync may have captured a partial response. Run a manual sync to refresh. If the discrepancy persists, click **Repair** in the data quality section of the Trade Republic panel to re-process imported data.

### QR code login appears to expire right after scanning

If Sure shows a login-expired or already-used error immediately after you scan the QR code in the Trade Republic app, start a new QR login from **Settings > Providers > Trade Republic**. The previous code was single-use and cannot be retried — the new code will work correctly.

### Phone number and PIN or password login confirmation times out

The push login confirmation window is open for approximately two minutes. Sure polls Trade Republic at one-second intervals and backs off automatically if Trade Republic is rate-limiting or temporarily unavailable. If the window closes before you confirm:

1. Click **Cancel** or wait for the panel to reset
2. Start a new login from **Settings > Providers > Trade Republic** and enter your PIN or password again

If the panel shows a rate-limit or outage alert, wait a moment and then click **Check login status** to retry manually. Sure will resume automatic polling on the next login attempt.

## Limitations

- **European accounts only**: Trade Republic operates in Europe. Accounts in unsupported regions cannot be connected.
- **Session expiry**: Long periods without a sync can cause the session to expire, requiring re-authentication.
- **Unpriced positions**: Some securities (particularly private market instruments) may not have a current price available from Trade Republic.
- **No options or derivatives**: Derivative instruments are not imported.
