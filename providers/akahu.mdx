Akahu is a New Zealand open banking platform. Sure connects to it using your personal app token and user token, syncing bank and investment account balances and transactions automatically.

<Note>
  Akahu support is in beta. Functionality is working but may have rough edges. Report issues in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/36ZGBsxYEK).
</Note>

## How it works

1. Create an Akahu account and register a personal app to get your app token and user token
2. Paste both tokens into Sure
3. Sure discovers your Akahu accounts and begins syncing balances and transactions
4. Choose how each discovered account should appear in Sure (Cash, Credit Card, Investment, or Loan)

## Prerequisites

- An [Akahu](https://akahu.nz/) account connected to your New Zealand bank accounts
- A personal app token and user token from the Akahu developer portal

## Setting up Akahu

### Get your tokens

1. Sign in to the [Akahu developer portal](https://developers.akahu.nz/)
2. Create a personal app — give it a name and description
3. Copy your **app token** (used to identify your application)
4. Follow the Akahu authorization flow to get your **user token** (grants access to your accounts)

### Connect Akahu in Sure

1. In Sure, go to **Settings > Bank sync**
2. Find the **Akahu** panel and enter your app token and user token
3. Click **Save** — Sure initiates a first sync to discover your accounts
4. Go to the **Accounts** tab and run account setup to choose how each Akahu account should appear

### Link discovered accounts

After the first sync, Sure shows all accounts returned by Akahu but not yet linked. For each one:

1. Choose an account type: **Cash**, **Credit Card**, **Investment**, or **Loan** — or skip accounts you do not want to track
2. Click **Create accounts** to create the selected accounts and begin syncing them

You can also link a discovered Akahu account to an existing Sure account if you already track that account manually.

## Supported account types

Akahu can return bank accounts, savings accounts, investment accounts, and credit cards. You choose how each one maps to a Sure account type during setup.

## Syncing

Sure syncs Akahu accounts on demand. Trigger a sync from the account card or provider panel at any time. Transactions include date, amount, and description, plus optional reference, particulars, and code fields when your bank provides them.

## Troubleshooting

**Connection shows "requires update"**: Your user token may have expired or been revoked. Re-enter the token in **Settings > Bank sync > Akahu**.

**Accounts not appearing after setup**: Trigger a manual sync from the Akahu provider panel. If accounts still do not appear, confirm your tokens are valid and that the accounts are visible in the Akahu portal.

**Transactions missing**: Akahu transactions are fetched using cursor-based pagination. A first sync retrieves recent history. Older history may not be available depending on your bank's data retention.

For Akahu API documentation, see [developers.akahu.nz](https://developers.akahu.nz/).
