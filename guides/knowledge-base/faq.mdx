## Transaction history and imports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How far back can Sure import transactions?">
    Available history depends on your bank, connection provider, and Sure's integration settings. Sure does not always request everything available: some integrations use a fixed initial date range or a record limit, and later syncs may only fetch recent changes.

    See [Transaction history and import limits](/providers/transaction-history) for the source-by-source comparison, defaults, and options for older history. Historical exports can help fill gaps; review overlapping dates to avoid duplicates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Balances and Pending Transactions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't my account balance include pending transactions?">
    Sure excludes pending transactions from account balance calculations. Account balances reflect only settled (posted) transactions.

    This prevents balances from being temporarily inflated or deflated by transactions that may still change amount, be cancelled, or be replaced by a posted version from your provider.

    Pending transactions remain visible in your transaction list. Once a transaction posts, it is counted in your balance and in reports.

    Providers that report pending status include SimpleFIN, Plaid, Lunchflow, Enable Banking, Akahu, Up, Mercury, and Redbark.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my balance look different from what my bank shows?">
    Banks commonly display an "available balance" that deducts pending transactions. Sure shows a settled balance — one that reflects only posted activity.

    If the difference matches the sum of your pending transactions, that is expected behavior. Your balance in Sure will update once those transactions post.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Transfers and Currencies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I have a transfer between accounts with different currencies (exchange rate)?">
    Yes, you can make transfers between accounts with different currencies. Sure automatically applies the exchange rate for the transfer date:

    - The outgoing transaction uses the source account's currency and amount.
    - The incoming transaction is converted to the destination account's currency using the rate for that date.
    - If no exchange rate is available for the transfer date, Sure raises a conversion error rather than falling back to a 1:1 rate. Make sure an exchange rate provider is configured before creating cross-currency transfers.
    - Foreign exchange metadata is recorded for auditing purposes.

    For best results, make sure an exchange rate provider is configured in **Settings → Self-Hosting** and rates are current.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when an account has transactions in a currency with no exchange rate?">
    When Sure has no stored rate at all for a currency pair, it leaves the affected amounts out of totals rather than counting them at a 1:1 rate. A warning appears at the top of the dashboard, Reports, and Transactions pages naming the currency so you know which totals are incomplete.

    When a rate exists but the newest stored rate is more than five days old, Sure uses that stored rate and shows an info notice with the currency name and the date of the rate being used.

    When a rate is missing only for a specific date but other stored rates exist for that pair, Sure uses the nearest stored rate — the latest one before that date, or the earliest one after it — rather than leaving the amount out.

    Once a current rate is available — after configuring or re-enabling an exchange rate provider and triggering a sync — the previously excluded amounts are included in the next calculation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
