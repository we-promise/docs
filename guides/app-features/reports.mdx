# Reports

The Reports page gives you a detailed breakdown of your income and spending over any time period. Use it to understand where your money goes, compare months, and spot large individual expenses.

## Filtering reports

### Filter by tag

You can filter the entire Reports page by one or more tags. This lets you see the full financial picture for a trip, project, renovation, or any other cross-category grouping you have tagged.

Open the filter controls at the top of the Reports page and select one or more tags. All sections on the page — Activity Breakdown, Biggest Expenses, and the income summary — update to show only transactions carrying those tags.

Tags are managed from [Transactions](/guides/app-features/transactions).

### Other filters

The Reports page also supports filtering by:

- **Accounts** — scope the report to one or more accounts
- **Category** — narrow to a specific category or subcategory
- **Amount range** — set minimum and maximum thresholds
- **Tax-advantaged exclusion** — exclude activity from tax-advantaged accounts
- **Finance account scoping** — include or exclude finance-only accounts

## Biggest Expenses

The **Biggest Expenses** section lists the largest individual transactions in the selected period, below the Activity Breakdown. The printed report includes the same list below Spending by Category.

**What is included:**

- Expenses only. Income transactions are excluded.
- The same filters as Activity Breakdown: tags, accounts, category, amount range, tax-advantaged exclusion, and finance-account scoping.
- Excluded transactions, transfers, and credit card payments are omitted.
- Investment contributions follow the family's **Count investing as spending** setting (configured in **Settings → Preferences**).
- One-time transactions are included and marked with a **One-time** pill, since those are often exactly the large individual charges you are looking for.

**Row count:** Use the **5 / 10 / 25** picker on the section heading to control how many expenses are shown. Your choice is saved per user and also applies to the printed report.

Rows link to the transaction drawer so you can review or edit the transaction directly.

## Activity Breakdown

The Activity Breakdown shows spending grouped by category for the selected period. Categories with subcategories can be expanded to see the detail. Transfers and excluded transactions are not included.

## Printed report

The printed report (accessible from the page actions menu) mirrors the web view. It includes the Spending by Category section and the Biggest Expenses list at the same row count you have selected on screen.
