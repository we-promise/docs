## Overview

Goals let you earmark money in your linked accounts toward specific targets. You can track a one-time savings target — a vacation, down payment, or new car — or maintain a standing reserve that you keep topped up at all times, like an emergency fund.

## Goal kinds

When you create a goal you choose its kind. The kind changes what 100% means, whether the goal can be closed, and how it appears in your list.

### One-off goal

A one-off goal has a finish line. Once you reach it, Sure offers to close it, which releases the earmarked money. You can set a target date and Sure will tell you whether your current savings pace will get you there on time.

**Statuses for one-off goals:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `reached` | Balance meets or exceeds the target — goal can be closed |
| `on_track` | Pace is sufficient to reach the target by the deadline |
| `behind` | Pace is not sufficient to reach the target by the deadline |
| `no_target_date` | Open-ended — no deadline set |

### Reserve to maintain

A reserve goal is a floor you hold, not an achievement you file away. An emergency fund is the typical example: once it is full you do not close it — you keep it there, and any withdrawal is a shortfall to rebuild.

A maintained reserve:

- Cannot be closed. Closing would release the earmarked money, which is the opposite of what a reserve is for. You can archive or pause it instead.
- Has no target date and no pace calculation. "Save $X per month to catch up" advice is not relevant when there is no deadline.
- Does not count toward the "goals on track" tile. A funded reserve is working exactly as intended; it is neither on track nor behind.

**Statuses for maintained reserves:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `funded` | Balance meets the target level — reserve is whole |
| `depleted` | Balance is below the target level — shortfall needs rebuilding |

A depleted reserve sorts alongside `behind` goals in your list and triggers a high-priority insight (see [Insights](/guides/app-features/insights)).

## Display order

Active goals appear in this order: goals needing attention first (behind or depleted), then on-track goals, then open-ended goals, then funded reserves. Paused goals sort last.

## Creating a goal

You can create a goal from the Goals page. Link at least one depository or investment account. The linked accounts are what Sure reads the balance from — no money moves automatically.

When you select "Reserve to maintain" the target date field is hidden. Any date stored from a previous kind is cleared.

## Linked accounts and earmarks

Each linked account can contribute either a fixed earmark (a specific dollar amount reserved for this goal) or its entire remaining balance after other goals' fixed earmarks.

Goals denominated in a currency different from their linked accounts are blocked at save time.

## Pausing and archiving

- **Pause** — stops the pace clock without releasing the earmark. A paused goal still reserves its money.
- **Archive** — releases the earmark. The accounts are free for other goals or general use.
- **Reopen** — moves a completed goal back to active. For a reserve, you must reopen it before you can change its kind.

## Pledges

A pledge records that you have saved or transferred money toward a goal. Sure matches it to the next qualifying transaction on the linked account and marks the pledge fulfilled. Unmatched pledges expire after a set window.

## Plan hub

The Plan hub shows a summary card across all active goals. The progress bar reflects the count of goals that need attention — behind one-offs and depleted reserves both count.
