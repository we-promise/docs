title: "AI assistant sidebar"
description: "Show, hide, and understand the AI assistant on desktop and mobile in Sure"
---

Sure includes an AI assistant on both desktop and mobile. On desktop it appears as a right-hand sidebar; on mobile it is accessible from the bottom navigation bar.

## Hide or show the AI sidebar on desktop

On desktop, use the **panel-right** button in the top bar to toggle the right-hand AI sidebar.

- Click it once to collapse the AI sidebar
- Click it again to reopen the AI sidebar
- Sure saves this preference to your account

This is useful if you want more room for the main app content or simply do not want to use the assistant yet.

## Accessing the assistant on mobile

On mobile, tap **Assistant** in the bottom navigation bar to open the AI chat interface.

If AI has not been enabled for your account yet, tapping Assistant shows the consent screen rather than the chat composer. This lets you review and accept the AI terms before starting a conversation. Once you accept, the full chat interface becomes available.

## Chat history in the native mobile app

The mobile app loads the full message history when you open a conversation. The chat API paginates messages in batches of 50, oldest first. The app fetches every page in sequence so long conversations render completely, with messages in chronological order and no duplicates.

If one of the intermediate pages fails to load — for example due to a network interruption — the app keeps the pages it already fetched instead of discarding the chat. The current screen does not display a partial-history notice. You can reopen the conversation to retry loading the missing messages.

## When the sidebar is hidden by default

Sure can also hide the AI sidebar by default for some people:

- New `member` and `guest` users start with the AI sidebar hidden when AI is not available
- `admin` users still see it by default so they can finish AI setup or troubleshooting

If AI becomes available later, you can reopen the sidebar at any time with the same desktop toggle.

## Chat titles

When you start a new chat without providing a custom title, Sure generates a default title using your current language and a timestamp. For example, "New chat 2026-08-31 14:30" in English, "Neuer Chat 2026-08-31 14:30" in German, or "Novo chat 2026-08-31 14:30" in Brazilian Portuguese. You can rename any chat at any time using the edit icon next to its title.

## Localized chat interface

The full chat interface is localized to match your language setting. This includes the AI greeting, suggested questions, chat navigation labels, consent dialog, and error messages. Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) is fully supported. Set your language under Settings → Preferences to switch the chat interface to your preferred language.

## What the assistant can see

The assistant has access to your account details, transactions, and holdings to answer questions about your finances.

### Account details

When you ask about an account, the assistant can read:

- Account name, type, subtype, and balance
- Institution domain and currency
- For loan accounts: interest rate, loan term in months, and rate type (fixed or variable), plus the calculated monthly payment
- For credit cards: APR, minimum payment, and annual fee

Fields the assistant reports as absent are fields you have not yet recorded in your account settings, not zeroes.

### Transactions

When you ask about transactions, the assistant can read:

- Amount (always a positive number), classification (income or expense), and date
- Category, merchant, tags, and notes
- Whether the transaction is pending or excluded from reports
- Transfer information: if the transaction is one leg of a transfer, the assistant can see the counterparty account when you have access to it

The assistant uses these fields to answer questions such as "how much did I spend on groceries last month" or "show me unmatched transfers". Because `amount` is always positive and direction lives in `classification`, the assistant avoids sign errors when summing income and expenses.

### Uncategorized transactions

When you ask the assistant to help categorize transactions, it groups uncategorized activity by payee rather than listing individual rows. It also tells you whether any categorization rules are currently active, so it can explain why transactions may still be uncategorized even after running rules.
