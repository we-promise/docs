---
title: Roadmap
description: What Sure is working on now, planning next, and exploring for the future
---

Sure's roadmap is organized into three phases. Items have one of three statuses: **In progress** means active work is happening now, **Planned** means the item is scoped and prioritized, and **Exploring** means the team is researching and gathering signal before committing.

## Short term

Stabilize and polish the core.

### Reliability, performance, and technical debt
**In progress** — Make the everyday experience dependable, fast, and easier to maintain.

### Web UX/UI polish
**Planned** — Refine the web experience around the details that make Sure feel clear and cohesive.

### AI controls and transparency
**Planned** — Give people understandable controls and visibility into how AI works with their data.

### Documentation and onboarding
**Planned** — Help new people get value from Sure quickly, whether they use the hosted app or self-host it.

### Mobile app planning and delivery
**Planned** — Establish and ship a thoughtful mobile experience for managing money on the go.

### Quality-focused release cadence
**Planned** — Build a sustainable rhythm for shipping improvements with confidence.

## Medium term

Expand personal-finance capability.

### First-class AI
**Exploring** — Make AI a useful, transparent part of the core financial workflow.

### Transaction intelligence
**Planned** — Turn transaction data into better organization, context, and decisions.

### Savings, retirement, and investments
**Planned** — Support more of the financial picture beyond day-to-day budgeting.

### Scenario simulation
**Exploring** — Help people understand the possible outcomes of financial choices before they make them.

### Localization and country support
**Planned** — Make Sure useful and natural for people across more countries, currencies, and financial systems.

### Themes and customization
**Planned** — Let people shape their financial workspace to fit the way they think and work.

## Long term

Open the platform carefully.

### External-agent integration and execution
**Exploring** — Create safe, permissioned ways for outside agents to work with Sure.

### Agent-assisted cleanup and memory
**Exploring** — Use agents to reduce financial-data maintenance while keeping people in control.

### Polished mobile ecosystem
**Planned** — Grow mobile into a complete, reliable part of the Sure experience.

### Improved macOS and desktop experience
**Planned** — Make Sure feel at home across desktop platforms and workflows.

### Business finance support
**Exploring** — Carefully extend Sure to the needs of people managing business finances.
